    import { connect, agent } from "@glc/core";

// Connect to what you already run. Nothing migrates.
const stack = await connect(
  ["salesforce", "gmail", "netsuite", "slack"],
  { access: "least-privilege", audit: true }
);

// One agent per team. People approve what matters.
await agent("sales-followups", {
  reads: [stack.salesforce, stack.gmail],
  creates: "draft follow-ups",
  approval: "account owner",
});

// status: "live", every action logged

    Executive search

    The right leader changes the trajectory. We find them.

    The best executives are rarely job hunting. They're running something important somewhere else. We use AI to map the entire market and read real track records, then apply the judgment only people can, so you meet a short slate of leaders built for the role you actually need filled.

    1. 01

      Calibrate the role

      We start with what this leader must own and deliver in their first 18 months, and turn it into a scorecard. Every candidate is measured against the same bar.

    2. 02

      Map the whole market

      AI maps operators at comparable companies, rising leaders one step from the role, and people who aren't looking. Nobody is missed because they never applied.

    3. 03

      Read the evidence

      We look past titles to what each person built: what grew under them, how their teams changed, what they shipped. Structured interviews are scored against the scorecard.

    4. 04

      Present a calibrated slate

      A short list, each with a written case for the hire, including the risks. You spend your time on finalists, not resumes.

    5. 05

      Close and land the hire

      Backchannel references, compensation benchmarking, offer strategy and a 90-day integration plan, so the hire works out after they sign.

    Where AI does the heavy lifting

    • Mapping every relevant leader, not just active candidates
    • Pulling signal from public work, talks, filings and company growth
    • Scoring every candidate against the same scorecard
    • Checking the slate for blind spots and bias

    Where people make the call

    • Reference and backchannel conversations
    • Culture, chemistry and leadership style
    • Negotiation and closing
    • Confidentiality, start to finish
