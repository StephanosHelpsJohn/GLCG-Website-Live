About GLC

Built to give people their time back.

We take the worst parts of the job off people's plates, so they can spend their hours on the work they actually love.

Founder & CEO

Stephan Nicklow

Stephan founded The Good Lyfe Consulting Group in 2019 with one goal: free people from soul-shattering work. Too many talented people lose their weeks to copy-paste tasks, status reports and manual handoffs.

Over the years GLC has run alongside the rest of his career. He worked in Big Tech at Meta, where he led Trust Strategy for the Metaverse, built and scaled platforms at 100M+ scale, and co-founded Gamerplug, an app that helps gamers find their people, where he is still CEO.

Now AI can finally do the work GLC was founded to eliminate, and GLC is front and center again. Stephan builds with generative AI every day and brings the same approach to every client: start with the people, find the work that drains them, and automate it inside the company, not just talk about it in meetings.

Our story

Big platforms want your data. We go to it.

Big AI platforms want your data to live on their systems. Most growing companies can't work that way. Their data is spread across a CRM, an ERP, spreadsheets, logs and a dozen other tools.

GLC connects to whatever you already use. That lets us build custom solutions around your real data, like agents that pull logs to catch errors early or analyze visual data, instead of forcing you onto a new platform.

We work with companies under 2,000 employees. They're big enough to feel the pain and small enough to move fast. We handle strategy, build custom AI agents for each business unit, train your team, stay on to monitor and support everything we ship, and help find the leaders to run it.
