GLC Privacy Policy

Our Commitment to Your Data

At The Good Lyfe Consulting Group (GLC), our mission is to free humanity from the cycle of soul-shattering work by helping you operationalize AI. A core part of this mission is our commitment to your privacy and the security of your business's data. We believe in building trust through our actions and have designed our processes to be Egoless, meaning we're focused on your success, not on collecting your data.

The Data We Access

Our services involve connecting to your company's data sources to create powerful AI solutions and workflows. We do not care where your source of truth lives—we can connect into any system as needed to pull data.

How We Handle Your Data

  • No Data Storage: We do not save or store your data on our servers. Our services are designed to process the information in place on your systems. This ensures your data remains under your control at all times.
  • Security: We maintain strict security protocols when accessing your systems. We work within your existing security framework to ensure that our access is limited, monitored, and fully aligned with your company's policies.
  • Purpose-Driven Access: We only access the specific data required to implement the AI strategies and workflows we've agreed upon. This could include visual data, operational logs, or other sources necessary to give your business a full picture of where it's headed.
  • No Third-Party Sharing: We will never share, sell, or distribute the data we access to any third parties.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy or our data handling practices, please contact us.
